# MediaDock — Legal Notice & Compliance

## 1. Intended Use
MediaDock is designed for lawful, personal use with authorized content:
- Downloading public domain audio and video.
- Downloading Creative Commons (CC) licensed media.
- Downloading content where the user holds copyright or explicit authorization from the rights holder.
- Archiving personal content uploaded to supported platforms.

## 2. Prohibition of DRM Circumvention
MediaDock does not contain circumvention devices, bypass keys, decryption routines, or software designed to evade technological protection measures (TPMs) as defined under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA, 17 U.S.C. § 1201), the EU Copyright Directive (Directive 2001/29/EC), or related national laws.

Requests for protected media streams encrypted with Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady, or similar digital rights management systems are not supported.
